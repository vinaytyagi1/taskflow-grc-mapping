## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes authentication standards to protect TaskFlow Software's systems, source code, and customer data from unauthorized access.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all full-time employees, contractors, and service accounts accessing TaskFlow corporate applications, cloud infrastructure (AWS), and source code repositories (GitHub).

## 3. Password Requirements
* **Minimum Length:** All user passwords must be at least **12 characters** long (16+ characters recommended for privileged accounts).
* **Complexity & Breached Passwords:** Passwords are checked against known breached password lists via Google Workspace and must not reuse the last 5 passwords.
* **Password Managers:** All staff are provisioned a company 1Password account; storing passwords in plaintext files, browsers, or Slack is prohibited.
* **Rotation:** Passwords are not forcibly rotated on a fixed schedule unless compromise is suspected, in alignment with NIST SP 800-63B guidance.

## 4. Single Sign-On (SSO) & Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
* **SSO Enforcement:** All supported SaaS applications (AWS, GitHub, Datadog, Jira, Slack) must authenticate through **Google Workspace SAML SSO**.
* **Mandatory MFA:** MFA is strictly enforced for 100% of users on Google Workspace and any standalone accounts.
* **Approved MFA Methods:** Hardware security keys (FIDO2/WebAuthn) or Time-based One-Time Password (TOTP) authenticator apps. SMS-based MFA is disabled due to SIM-swapping risks.
